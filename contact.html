Contact Us

Reach out to the Cellulum editorial team for academic questions, errata reports, or digital resource support.

Have a question regarding a biochemical derivation, spotted a calculation error in a solved CSIR NET problem, or need assistance with a study bundle download? We welcome feedback from students, educators, and life sciences researchers.

Direct Contact Channels

  • Editorial & General Inquiries: cellulum.in@gmail.com
  • Main Interactive Observatory: https://cellulum.in
  • Study Bundles & Community Support: topmate.io/cellulum

What You Can Reach Out About

  • Errata & Equation Corrections: If you notice a typographical error in a LaTeX equation, derivation step, or CSIR NET answer key, please include the article title and specific section number in your email so we can verify and update it promptly.
  • Topic Requests: Suggest specific CSIR NET Life Sciences Part B or Part C topics, biophysical methods, or quantitative problem sets you would like to see covered next.
  • Digital Download Support: If you purchased a high-yield study pack or supported us via Topmate (Buy Us a Chai) and experienced any issue accessing your PDF, email us with your transaction reference for immediate resolution.

We typically respond to all student and academic inquiries within 24–48 hours.
