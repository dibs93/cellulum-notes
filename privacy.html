Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

At Cellulum Notes (accessible from https://notes.cellulum.in) and our main interactive observatory (https://cellulum.in), protecting the privacy of our visitors is a top priority. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of information collected and recorded by our website and how we use it.

1. Log Files & Analytics

Cellulum Notes follows a standard procedure of using server log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites—a standard practice across all hosting providers (including GitHub Pages and Cloudflare). The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, and improving our educational study guides.

2. Cookies, Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve advertisements to our site visitors based upon their visit to notes.cellulum.in, cellulum.in, and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising and the use of DART cookies at any time by visiting the Google Ads Settings page. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Cellulum Notes's Privacy Policy does not apply to other advertisers or external websites. You can choose to disable cookies through your individual browser options. More detailed information about cookie management with specific web browsers can be found at the browsers' respective websites.

4. External Links, Digital Downloads & Payment Processing

Our website contains links to our main interactive platform (https://cellulum.in) as well as trusted third-party services for educational downloads and creator support:

  • Google Drive: Free CSIR NET question papers and reference PDFs are hosted on Google Drive for direct viewing and download.
  • Topmate: Voluntary community support ("Buy Us a Chai") and premium study bundle purchases are processed securely through Topmate. Cellulum Notes does not collect, process, or store any banking, card, or UPI payment details on its servers.

5. Consent & Contact Information

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms. If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, please contact us via our Contact Page or email us directly at cellulum.in@gmail.com.
