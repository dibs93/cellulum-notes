About Cellulum Notes

Independent quantitative life sciences guides, step-by-step biochemical derivations, and analytical problem-solving resources.

Cellulum Notes (notes.cellulum.in) is the open-access editorial companion to the Cellulum Interactive Observatory. Our mission is to make quantitative life sciences, molecular biology, biochemistry, and competitive exam preparation accessible through rigorous first-principles derivations and interactive visual modeling.

What We Cover

Many life sciences resources treat biology as pure memorization, leaving students unprepared for quantitative and experimental design questions in competitive examinations such as CSIR NET Life Sciences (Part B & Part C), GATE Biotechnology / Life Sciences, DBT-JRF, and ICMR-JRF. Cellulum bridges that gap by focusing on:

  • Biochemistry & Bioenergetics: Step-by-step derivations of Michaelis-Menten enzyme kinetics, reversible inhibition plots, Gibbs free energy ($\Delta G^{\circ\prime}$), and redox potentials.
  • Genetics & Evolution: Three-point testcross linkage mapping, pedigree probability logic, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, and population genetics numericals.
  • Molecular Biology & Recombinant DNA Technology: Vector-insert molar ligation ratios, blue-white screening troubleshooting, restriction mapping, and real-time qPCR ($2^{-\Delta\Delta C_t}$) quantification.
  • Biophysical Methods & Ecology: UV-Vis spectrophotometry (Beer-Lambert law), Scatchard binding plots, Nernst membrane potentials, logistic population growth models, and community diversity indices.

Editorial Standards & Originality

Every study guide, mathematical derivation, and experimental breakdown published on Cellulum Notes is originally authored and verified by biotechnology and molecular biology researchers. Equations are typeset for clarity using MathJax, and complex pathways are paired with live interactive calculators and multi-scale visualizers hosted on our main platform at cellulum.in.
