About Cellulum Notes

Cellulum Notes (`notes.cellulum.in`) is the editorial companion publication to the Cellulum Interactive Observatory. Our mission is to make quantitative life sciences, molecular biology, biochemistry, and analytical exam preparation accessible through rigorous derivations and visual learning.

Editorial Standards

All study guides, mathematical derivations, and experimental breakdowns published on Cellulum Notes are originally authored by biotechnology and molecular biology researchers to help CSIR NET, GATE, and university life sciences students master complex concepts from first principles.
